      Contractors

      A contractor can be on many families. A family has one contractor, or none. The number is the contractor id a CSV uses.

        Add a family

        They land in the first column. Leave the contractor blank when there is not one yet.

        Upload a CSV

        One header row, then one family per line. Column names: first_name, last_name, address_1, address_2, city, state, zip, email, cell_phone, opt_in_call, opt_in_email, originated_at, notes, contractor_id. The same columns can be written as first name, last name, address 1, address 2, city, state, zip, email address, cell phone, opt in for phone call, opt in for email, date of originating record creation, notes, and contractor internal id. Opt in is yes or no. Dates are YYYY-MM-DD. Leave contractor id blank when there is not one. Up to 200 families at a time.
