    Questions to ask here

    Bring these to a contractor, a guide, or an Ask Anything 1:1.

    • Can the path be graded so there's no step at all?
    • How long will the ramp need to be?
    • Will there be rails on both sides of the stairs?
    • What happens to the stairlift in a power cut?
    • Is there room for a landing at the top and bottom?
    • Does the door threshold need lowering?
