    Questions families ask here

    Bring these to any contractor, guide or family meeting.

    • What is this tile's DCOF rating when wet?
    • Will the flooring be smooth for a walker's wheels?
    • Is the finish matte or glossy?
    • What color temperature are the bulbs?
    • Can every handle be used with a closed fist?
    • Is there contrast at stair edges and counter edges?
