    Questions to ask here

    Bring these to a contractor, a guide, or an Ask Anything 1:1.

    • Does this project need a permit?
    • Who will pull the permit and schedule inspections?
    • Does the HOA need to approve exterior changes?
    • Is the contractor licensed and insured here?
    • Will the work be inspected before walls are closed?
    • Can I have a copy of the final inspection?
