      Questions to ask here

      Bring these to a contractor, or to the next Live Q&A.

      • Will this change appeal to most buyers here?
      • Is this the only tub in the house?
      • Can the change be reversed easily?
      • Are we keeping receipts, permits and photos?
      • Would a local agent see this as an upgrade?
      • Does this add square footage or just change it?
