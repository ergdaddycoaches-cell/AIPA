    Questions families ask here

    Bring these to any contractor, guide or family meeting.

    • Will the shower have any lip or step at all?
    • Where will the wood blocking for grab bars go?
    • What is the DCOF rating of the floor tile?
    • Can the sink be used from a seat?
    • How will water be kept away from the door?
    • Will the toilet be comfort height?
