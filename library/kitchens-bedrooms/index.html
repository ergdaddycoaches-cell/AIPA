      Questions to ask here

      Bring these to a contractor, or to the next Live Q&A.

      • Can everyday items live between knee and shoulder height?
      • Is there a room on the main floor that could become a bedroom?
      • Will the cooktop turn itself off?
      • Is the path from bed to bathroom lit at night?
      • Could the ceiling support a lift later?
      • Are there trip hazards along the main routes?
