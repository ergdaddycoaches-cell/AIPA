    Questions families ask here

    Bring these to any contractor, guide or family meeting.

    • What's included in the quote, and what isn't?
    • Is a permit included in the price?
    • Does a doctor or occupational therapist need to recommend the work?
    • Does the program pay the contractor, or reimburse you?
    • Is there a waiting list?
    • Can the work be done in stages to spread the cost?
