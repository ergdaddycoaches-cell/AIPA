Why the ADA doesn't cover your home, and why we use it anyway

The ADA applies to public places, not a private house. Its measurements are still the ones worth using.

A restaurant has to follow the ADA. Your house does not. The measurements in that law are still the best-tested ones for a doorway, a toilet, and a path through a room.
